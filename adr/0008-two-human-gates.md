# ADR-0008: Two human gates, automatic acceptance

Status: Proposed
Date: 2026-11-20
Deciders: Bernardo Manfriani, Gianmarco Ferruzzi

## Context
Humans must stay accountable without becoming a bottleneck.

## Decision
Gate 1: approve contract and capabilities. Gate 2: accept evidence; automatic when every threshold is observed and no capability changed.

## Consequences
Humans act at the start and the end, never in the middle.
